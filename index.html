Disciplines & Craft

Foundational Engineering Pillars

Every layer of development is architected with semantic precision, strict keyboard ergonomics, and benchmarked performance.

Semantic HTML5 & Living Standards

Structuring content strictly with native elements (<header>, <nav>, <main>, <section>, <article>, <aside>, <footer>) to preserve document hierarchy and provide intrinsic assistive technology cues.

WCAG 2.2 AA & AAA Compliance

Proactive accessibility audits adhering to W3C standards: automated continuous integration via axe-core, manual keyboard testing, screen reader flows (NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver), and focus ring visibility.

Design System Architecture

Building composable, tokenized component libraries with high-contrast color palettes, accessible focus states, ARIA APG design patterns, and zero third-party framework bloat.

SEO & Performance Engineering

Clean structured metadata, Open Graph & Twitter cards, JSON-LD schemas, sub-second Core Web Vitals (LCP, INP, CLS), and responsive fluid layouts that adapt seamlessly to every device.
