Frequently Asked Questions

Key questions regarding consultation engagements, accessibility compliance, and audits.

What is included in an Enterprise Accessibility Audit?
An enterprise audit evaluates your digital product across automated linters (axe-core, Pa11y), manual keyboard testing (tab stops, focus traps, visual indicators), and live screen reader testing with NVDA, JAWS, and VoiceOver. You receive an itemized remediation backlog paired with runnable code patches and an executive compliance summary.
How do you guarantee a 100/100 Lighthouse Accessibility and SEO score?
We achieve 100/100 by adhering strictly to W3C semantic HTML5 living standards, ensuring all landmarks are distinct, eliminating contrast regressions with mathematical color tokens, providing accessible names for all interactive elements, and structuring comprehensive JSON-LD schemas and SEO meta tags.
Can you author VPAT (Voluntary Product Accessibility Templates) / ACR reports?
Yes. As an IAAP-certified accessibility specialist, I author official VPAT 2.4 / Accessibility Conformance Reports (ACR) aligned with Section 508 (US Federal), EN 301 549 (European Union), and WCAG 2.2 AA standards required for enterprise procurement and government contracts.
What is your typical engagement model and turnaround time?
Engagements range from 2-week rapid diagnostic audits to multi-month embedded staff architecture roles for design system migrations. Initial consultation calls can typically be scheduled within 3 to 5 business days of contact submission.
